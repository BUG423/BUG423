### 👨‍💻 关于我

我是厦门大学硕士研究生，研究方向为**人工智能与惯性定位**。我的核心研究聚焦于数据驱动的惯性里程计（Inertial Odometry）、非线性复杂运动条件下的鲁棒轨迹估计，以及端侧轻量化深度学习与多传感器融合算法。

同时，我拥有从底层电路硬件设计（立创EDA硬件量产）、固件驱动开发到上层图形化低代码平台与全栈应用的工程实现能力。

- 🎓 **学历背景**：
  - **2023.09 – 至今** · 厦门大学信息学院，通信工程，**硕士（保研）**
  - **2019.09 – 2023.06** · 厦门大学电子科学与技术学院，电子信息科学与技术，**工学学士**
- 🔬 **研究重点**：神经网络惯性里程计、多模态时频特征融合、端侧移动端位姿解算
- 🛠️ **核心技术栈**：Python, C/C++, PyTorch, Linux, Git, Docker, ESP32, 树莓派, 立创EDA
- 🤝 **学术与技术交流**：欢迎围绕惯性导航、定位算法、嵌入式智能开展学术交流与项目合作！邮箱：[1264696035@qq.com](mailto:1264696035@qq.com)

## 🇬🇧 English

### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an M.Eng. student at Xiamen University, majoring in **Artificial Intelligence and Inertial Positioning**. My research focuses on data-driven inertial odometry (IO), robust trajectory estimation under non-linear and high-dynamic motion, on-device lightweight neural network architectures, and multi-sensor fusion.

Beyond algorithm research, I possess full-stack embedded hardware capability spanning PCB circuit design (mass production), low-level firmware drivers, and cross-platform low-code/web applications.

- 🎓 **Education**:
  - **2023.09 – Present** · M.Eng. in Communication Engineering (Recommended for Admission), School of Informatics, Xiamen University
  - **2019.09 – 2023.06** · B.Eng. in Electronic Information Science and Technology, School of Electronic Science and Engineering, Xiamen University
- 🔬 **Interests**: Neural Inertial Odometry, Time-Frequency Representation Learning, On-Device AI
- 🛠️ **Skills**: Python, C/C++, PyTorch, Linux, Git, Docker, ESP32, Raspberry Pi, EasyEDA
